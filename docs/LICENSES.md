## Cursor packs that ship with Cursed

Eighty-three packs are built into the installer and unpacked the first time you
run it. They are the catalog.

### Geared Brass and Geared Steel — GPL-3.0

These two are by **piraker-grinor** and are licensed under the **GNU General
Public License, version 3**, with the author stating the artwork is their own
work.

Their licence travels with them. Each pack's own `LICENSE.txt` and
`COPYRIGHT.txt` are unpacked into `%APPDATA%\Cursed\imported\` beside the cursor
files, so the full text of the GPL is on your machine wherever those cursors
are. They sit alongside Cursed as separately-licensed data rather than as part
of it — what the GPL calls mere aggregation. Nothing on this page claims any
right over them.

### The other eighty-one

Thirty-four came from cursor-sharing sites and state no licence. Forty-seven
were given to the project directly by the person who drew them, for use here,
with no credit asked for.

Several across both groups depict characters, logos or products belonging to
other people. Permission from whoever drew a cursor is not permission from
whoever owns what it depicts, and no claim is made over any of it. If you own
something one of these depicts and want it gone, it is one entry in one list —
say so and it goes.
