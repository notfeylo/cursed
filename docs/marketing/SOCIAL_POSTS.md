## Product post

Windows has 17 pointer roles. Changing only the arrow leaves the link hand, text
cursor, busy cursor, and resize pointers behind.

Cursed applies a complete matching set, includes 83 packs, and can turn your own
image or GIF into a native Windows cursor. Free and open source.

https://www.trycursed.com/

## Image-to-cursor post

The part of a cursor that actually clicks is its hotspot. If that point is wrong,
the artwork can look perfect while every click feels off.

Here is a practical guide to preparing an image, choosing the hotspot, and making
a real Windows cursor:

https://www.trycursed.com/guides/image-to-cursor

## Technical post

A PNG renamed to .cur is not a Windows cursor. CUR stores a hotspot and can carry
multiple resolutions; ANI adds frames and timing for animation.

We wrote down the useful differences, including why scaling matters:

https://www.trycursed.com/guides/cursor-file-formats
