# Privacy Policy

**Cursed** — last updated 2026-08-28.

## Cursed collects nothing

No analytics. No telemetry. No crash reporting. No account. No identifiers. No
profiling. Nothing about you or your computer is measured, stored anywhere else,
or sent anywhere.

## One request, and you can switch it off

Cursed makes a single kind of network request: an **update check**, to see
whether a newer version exists. It sends nothing but the request itself — no
identifier, no information about you or your machine.

Turn it off under **Settings → General → Check for updates automatically** and
Cursed makes no network requests at all. Everything else — the cursors, the
fonts, the background removal, these documents — already works offline.

## Everything stays on your computer

Your settings, your saved cursors, the cursors you import and the rendered
cursor files all live in `%APPDATA%\Cursed`, on your own machine. You can open
that folder from **Settings → Advanced** at any time.

## Nothing you import is ever transmitted

Images and cursor files you bring in are read on your computer, turned into
cursors on your computer, and stay there. They are never uploaded, and they are
never included in anything Cursed ships.

## Removing it removes everything

Uninstalling puts your original pointers back and then deletes what Cursed
created — and it asks first whether you would rather keep the cursors you made.
Deleting `%APPDATA%\Cursed` yourself has the same effect immediately.

If optional telemetry is ever added it will be **off unless you turn it on**,
anonymous, and written here before the release that contains it.
