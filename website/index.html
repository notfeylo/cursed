About

A simpler way to make Windows yours

Cursed is a Windows cursor customizer for changing every system pointer from one place. Choose from 83 included cursor packs or turn a picture or animated GIF into a Windows cursor. It applies a complete set across all 17 Windows pointer roles, so the arrow, link hand, text cursor, busy cursor, and resize pointers match. You can adjust size and color, crop imported images, keep animated images moving, and choose what appears when you hover over a link. Cursed remembers your choice after Windows theme changes and gives you a one-click way to restore your previous cursor scheme. It runs without an account, administrator access, ads, or telemetry.

Privacy

Cursed does not require an account and does not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or personal information. Your settings, presets, imported images, and generated cursor files stay on your computer and are never uploaded by Cursed.

If automatic update checks are enabled, the app makes a request to GitHub to see whether a newer release is available. That request contains no Cursed account or device identifier, and you can disable it in Settings. Visiting this website or downloading an installer may still give Vercel or GitHub the ordinary technical information needed to serve the request under their own privacy policies.

This website has no cookies, analytics, advertising, sign-in, contact form, or third-party tracking scripts. If Cursed ever adds optional telemetry, it will be off unless you enable it and this policy will be updated before that release.
